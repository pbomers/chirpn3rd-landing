What ties them together

Every design below is a Y2K interface element that has been quietly hijacked by a car joke. Nothing needs new illustration work: they reuse the chickadee, the winged crest, the shifter gate badge, and the CH3RP wordmark you already own.

How I'd play it

If the drop needs a hero, it is You've Got Chirp. It carries the rebrand in four words, works on a sticker and a hoodie without redrawing, and is the one that explains the whole Chirpn3rd Online idea to someone who has never seen the site.

The Garage Buddy List is the sleeper. It is the only design here that people will stand and read, and every name on it is a conversation with the person wearing it. It also doubles as free advertising for the site, since the site is literally that window.

For a first drop I would cut it to four: You've Got Chirp, Buddy List, Illegal Operation, and brb shifting as the low-key everyday piece. That covers loud, funny, nerdy, and wearable without splitting the print budget eight ways.
